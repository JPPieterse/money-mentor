### Filing

## Tax-Free Savings Account (TFSA)

- **Annual limit: R46,000 per TAX YEAR (1 Mar – end Feb)** — from 1 Mar 2026 (Budget 2026; was R36,000 from 2021).
  ⚠ NOT a calendar year. Contributions count to the tax year in which they're made.
- **Lifetime limit: R500,000** (unchanged), aggregated across ALL your TFSAs. SARS tracks via provider reporting.
- **Penalty: 40% on excess contributions** — and withdrawals do NOT restore contribution room. Withdraw R10k, you cannot "put it back" without it counting again.
- **Moving providers:** use the official TFSA transfer process (allowed since 2018). Withdraw-and-recontribute burns lifetime limit.
- **Eligible:** bank savings/fixed deposits, SA-registered ETFs and unit trusts (including global-exposure feeders), money market. **Not eligible:** direct offshore accounts, derivatives, margin.
- All interest, dividends and capital gains inside are tax-free; nothing is reported on your return.
- Best use: long-horizon compounding (equities ETFs) — using it as a parking account wastes the lifetime limit.

## Retirement Funds (RA / Pension / Provident)

### Deduction
- **27.5% of the greater of remuneration or taxable income**, capped at:
  - **R430,000/year from 1 Mar 2026** (2027 tax year — Budget 2026 raised it, first change since 2016)
  - **R350,000/year for the 2026 tax year and earlier**
- Excess contributions are NOT lost — they carry forward to future years (and ultimately reduce lump-sum/annuity tax).
- Contributions reduce taxable income now; growth inside the fund is untaxed.

### Two-Pot Retirement System (since 1 Sep 2024 — applies to pension, provident, RA)
- Contributions split: **1/3 → savings pot** (accessible), **2/3 → retirement pot** (locked to retirement, must be annuitised).
- Pre-Sep-2024 balances sit in a **vested pot** under old rules. One-off seeding of the savings pot: 10% of the 31 Aug 2024 balance, max R30,000.
- **Savings pot withdrawals:** one per tax year, minimum R2,000, **taxed at your marginal rate** plus admin fee — expensive money; emergencies only.
- Retirement pot: no access before retirement, then must buy an annuity (subject to the de minimis below).

### At retirement (55+ for RAs)
- Up to 1/3 as lump sum; the rest annuitised — UNLESS total fund interest ≤ **R360,000** (de minimis, from 1 Mar 2026; was R247,500) in which case full commutation is allowed.
- **Retirement lump-sum tax (2026 tax year table — verify before relying):**
  0% up to R550,000 · 18% on 550,001–770,000 · 27% on 770,001–1,155,000 · 36% above.
  **⚠ The R550,000 tax-free amount is a LIFETIME CUMULATIVE total across ALL retirement funds and all withdrawals/severance benefits — NOT per product.** Prior withdrawals eat into it.
- Living annuity: drawdown 2.5%–17.5%/yr; can commute fully if value falls below R150,000 (from 1 Mar 2026).
- Preservation funds: one withdrawal allowed before retirement from the vested portion (pre-two-pot rules); post-two-pot flows follow pot rules.

## Medical Tax Credits (s6A/6B)

- **2027 tax year:** R376/month each for first two beneficiaries, R254/month each additional (verified).
- **2026 tax year:** R364 / R364 / R246.
- These are **fixed rand credits** against tax owed — NOT a percentage of contributions.
- **Additional medical expenses credit (s6B):** 65+/disability: 33.3% of excess contributions + qualifying out-of-pocket; others: 25% of the amount exceeding 7.5% of taxable income.

## Interest, Dividends, Donations

- **Interest exemption:** R23,800/yr (<65), R34,500 (65+) — unchanged for years.
- **Dividends withholding tax: 20%** (SA dividends; withheld at source). Foreign dividends: s10B partial inclusion, effective max ~20%.
- **Donations tax:** 20% on cumulative donations to R30M, 25% above. **Annual exemption R150,000 from 1 Mar 2026** (was R100,000). Spouse-to-spouse donations exempt **only if recipient spouse is SA tax resident** (anti-avoidance, from 25 Feb 2026).

## Estate Essentials (what the estate-planning skill must know for SA)

- **Estate duty:** 20% on dutiable estate to R30M, 25% above. **Abatement: R3.5M**, portable to a surviving spouse (effectively up to R7M on the second death). Property to a surviving spouse deducts fully (s4(q)).
- **Executor's fee:** up to 3.5% + VAT of gross assets — negotiable; name it in the will.
- **⚠ s37C Pension Funds Act:** retirement fund death benefits do NOT simply follow your beneficiary nomination — trustees must identify and consider all financial dependants (nomination is guidance, not binding). Life policies DO pay nominated beneficiaries directly, bypassing the estate (and avoid executor fees).
- **Marriage regime matters enormously:**
  - **In community of property:** one joint estate — spouses share assets AND debts; creditors can attack the joint estate; both spouses' consent needed for credit agreements.
  - **Out of community with accrual** (default for post-1984 ANC marriages): separate estates during marriage, growth shared on dissolution.
  - **Out of community without accrual:** fully separate.
- Wills: 2 competent witnesses (who are not beneficiaries), every page signed; Master of the High Court administers deceased estates. Dying intestate = Intestate Succession Act distribution + court-appointed guardianship complications for minors.

## Offshore Allowances (Budget 2026)

- **Single Discretionary Allowance: R2M per calendar year** (was R1M) — no tax clearance needed.
- Foreign Investment Allowance: R10M/yr with SARS tax-compliance (AIT) clearance.
- Cross-border card transaction cap: R100,000 per transaction (was R50,000).

## National Credit Act (NCA) — Debt Rules (corrected)

### Early settlement
- **Original principal ≤ R250,000:** settle ANY time with no penalty. Settlement amount = balance + interest to settlement date.
- **Original principal > R250,000 (most vehicle finance, bonds):** the creditor MAY charge an early-termination fee of up to ~3 months' interest — **avoidable by giving 3 months' written notice** of intent to settle.
- **Settlement quotes:** creditor must provide on request; valid 5 business days. Always settle off a written quote.

### Default & enforcement
- **s129 notice:** before legal action, the creditor must send a default notice and give you 10+ business days (and the debt must be 20+ business days in arrears). This is the "letter before action" — never ignore it; it offers debt-review/ombud routes.
- **s103(5) "statutory in duplum":** while in default, the total of interest + fees + collection costs cannot exceed the unpaid balance at the time of default — a hard cap on how much a defaulted debt can balloon.
- **Prescription:** most unsecured debts prescribe after 3 years of no payment/acknowledgement/summons. Acknowledging or paying ANY amount restarts the clock. Prescribed debt cannot lawfully be revived by collectors.
- **Debt review (s86):** statutory restructuring via a registered debt counsellor; legal protection while in process, but you cannot take new credit and exiting requires a clearance certificate (all short-term debt settled). Last resort — it locks you in.

### Cost-of-credit caps (why SA unsecured debt is expensive)
Max interest rates are repo-linked (verify current repo at resbank.co.za). Indicative caps: unsecured personal loans ≈ repo + 21% (high-20s% in practice — typical real-world unsecured loans run 18–29%), credit cards ≈ repo + 14%, store cards similar, short-term loans (≤6 months, ≤R8k) up to 5%/month on a first loan — this is why payday-style loans (Wonga etc.) are wealth-destroyers even when "fees look small".
Credit life insurance on NCA loans is capped (unsecured credit: R4.50/month per R1,000 outstanding) — check you're not overpaying; you may substitute your own compliant policy.

## Home Office Deduction (salaried remote employees)

Deductible under s11(a) read with s23(b)/(m) if: a dedicated part of the home is used **exclusively and regularly** for work AND >50% of duties are performed there. Deduction = floor-area % of rent/bond interest + utilities, plus s11(e) wear-and-tear on equipment (items <R7,000 each: full write-off in year of use — SARS IN47). Home office claims are a known **audit/verification trigger** — keep lease, floor plan, employer letter confirming remote work, and receipts.
