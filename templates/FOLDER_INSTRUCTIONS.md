# Folder Instructions

This folder contains a personal finance management system powered by Money Mentor.

## What this folder is for
- Tracking household income, expenses, and debts
- Managing a debt elimination strategy (hybrid snowball/avalanche)
- Generating monthly dashboards and payment plans
- Planning for wealth building after debt freedom

## How to use it
1. Say "process [month] finances" to run the monthly workflow
2. Drop bank statements into statements/[name]/[year]/
3. Drop creditor statements into statements/loans/[name]/
4. The system reads references/ for your household data and strategy
5. Results go into dashboards/ and archive/

## Key files
- `references/household-data.md` — your complete financial profile
- `references/repayment-strategy.md` — your debt payoff plan
- `spreadsheets/FinPlan.xlsx` — budget and debt tracker
- `dashboards/progress_dashboard.pptx` — monthly progress dashboard

## Privacy
All your personal data stays in this folder on your computer. The Money Mentor skills read from these files on demand — nothing is hardcoded or stored elsewhere.
