# Debt Repayment Strategy

## Approach: Hybrid Snowball/Avalanche

We use a hybrid strategy that balances psychological momentum (snowball) with mathematical efficiency (avalanche):

- **Phase 1 — Quick Kills**: Small debts that can be cleared in 1-3 months. These free up minimum payments quickly and build momentum.
- **Phase 2 — Mid-Tier**: Moderate balances that take 3-6 months. Continue building the cascade.
- **Phase 3 — Expensive Debt**: Highest interest rates with larger balances. This is where the most interest savings happen.
- **Phase 4 — Structured Debt**: Lowest rates, longest terms (vehicles, mortgage). These are last because the rates are manageable and terms are long.

Within each phase, attack the smallest balance first (snowball) unless there's a significant rate difference (>5%) between debts in the same phase — then attack the higher rate first (avalanche).

### Aggression Guidelines
- All salary increases go to the snowball — no lifestyle creep
- All freed minimums go to the next target — no "rewarding ourselves"
- One discretionary event per month maximum during active payoff
- If there's a windfall (bonus, tax refund, gift), at least 80% goes to the snowball

### Accountability
- Monthly processing: parse statements, reconcile, update plan (10-minute routine)
- Track interest burn rate vs baseline — this number should drop every month
- Celebrate milestones: each cleared debt is a win worth acknowledging
